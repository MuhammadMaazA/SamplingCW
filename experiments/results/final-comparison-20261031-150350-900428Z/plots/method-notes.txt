Frame failures include all planned trials, including missing and truncated frames.
Channel BER uses observed frame bits; decoded BER uses complete decodable trials.
Both BERs are conditional on observation/decodability; read them with failure counts.
Throughput uses nominal frame airtime, excluding gaps and setup.
Counts and rates are descriptive; they do not establish statistical significance.
Unavailable tails indicate sampling stopped; later bits were not evaluated.
Noise maps show at most 20 trials and 2000 bits; error-position CSVs retain all errors.
